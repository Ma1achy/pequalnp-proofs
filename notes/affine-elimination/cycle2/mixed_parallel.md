# Five-clause encodings with two parallel helper lines

For the even inner product on six visible bits, consider five forbidden affine flats with two helper bits and no cylinders. A line leaf forbids an affine line of the helper square on a visible affine domain, and a point leaf forbids a singleton on its domain. Two parallel line leaves have the same fixed direction but may change their offsets independently as affine functions of the visible input.

**Claim.** None of the three candidate mixed profiles with a parallel pair can represent the predicate. The profiles have respectively two parallel lines and three points, two parallel lines with a line of another direction and two points, or two parallel lines with one line of each other direction and one point. This exclusion concerns those profiles. A complementary profile with one line has a five-clause construction.

The odd visible set has 28 points. A nonempty visible affine flat lying entirely in the odd set has at most four points. A visible domain of codimension at least two contains at most ten odd points; a proper hyperplane contains at most sixteen. These bounds follow from the Walsh sums or direct finite enumeration. A full-domain line would fix the surviving value of one affine helper coordinate everywhere. Substitution would leave at most four affine clauses on one helper, impossible since the exact one-helper optimum is six. [Finite geometric bounds](synthesis.md)

Label the parallel lines 0 and 1, and the three other leaves 2, 3 and 4. A minimal square-cover certificate gives one visible affine conflict for each of the following shapes. There is the pair 01. There are the six triples consisting of one of 0,1 and two of 2,3,4. In the second and third profiles there is also the triple 234. These are all candidates. Complementary parallel lines cover the square by themselves. If they coincide at a visible point then one cannot be necessary in a minimal certificate containing both. Two nonparallel lines and a singleton must cover the fourth point. Three distinct line directions must concur. A line and two singletons must put the two singletons at the two points off the line. Each condition is a conjunction of affine equations on the active domains. A family of three singletons cannot cover the helper square. [Certificate shapes and offset checks](synthesis.py)

For either fixed parallel line, at most four certificates avoid that line. Their union contains at most sixteen odd points, so its domain must contain at least twelve odd points. Thus both parallel lines have hyperplane domains. Their pair 01 can never conflict: its locus is the intersection of their two hyperplanes with the one equation requiring unequal line offsets. If nonempty this would have dimension at least three and at least eight points, even though every point of the locus must be odd.

The first profile now has only six candidate flats for 28 odd points, which is impossible. The other two profiles have exactly seven. Every one must be a four-point odd plane, and all seven must be mutually disjoint. Each leaf among 2,3,4 appears in four triples involving one parallel line and in triple 234. Its domain therefore contains at least twenty odd points from those five planes. It must be the full visible domain. At least one of these three leaves is itself a line, contradicting the full-domain-line exclusion.
